# Track: Quant (researcher / trader / developer, energy and commodity trading)

Edit this file to match the roles you apply for.

## Target roles and employers (examples; verify each firm's current programmes)
- **Energy and commodity trading / analysis:** power producers' trading and analysis teams. A natural bridge for engineering students (optimisation, forecasting, production planning).
- **Quant roles at asset managers, pension funds and banks.**
- **Proprietary trading firms:** quant trader / researcher internships. Heavily test-based.
- **Quant developer:** more weight on C++/Python engineering.

Timing: many quant internships open in summer/autumn for the following summer and close early.

## What they screen for (ranked)
1. Quantitative ability: probability, statistics, linear algebra; tested in online assessments.
2. Programming: Python for research, C++ for developers.
3. Evidence of applied quantitative work: models on noisy data, careful validation, honest results.
4. Grades: transcripts are almost always requested; math courses are scrutinised.
5. Genuine interest in markets and a credible "why this field" story.
6. Competitions and problem-solving track record.
